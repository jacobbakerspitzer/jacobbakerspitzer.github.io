About / Skills

Raised on New York courts and grids. Now building circuits and code in Durham.

I'm a first-year Electrical & Computer Engineering student at Duke with a strong interest in economics. I grew up in New York City and went to NEST+m, and the city made me curious about how systems work: circuits, software, buildings, markets, the grid under Manhattan.

Before Duke, I compiled a 1,000+ building dataset covering about a century of NYC construction for an economics research project, and spent a week shadowing a structural engineer at The LiRo Group. Now I'm on an EGR 101 team designing a two-switch game for a young client, while building up Python, C++, CAD and circuits. Outside class it's sports and music, from refereeing 180+ soccer matches to baritone sax. Every one of those has a rulebook, and I like learning the rules well enough to build inside them.
